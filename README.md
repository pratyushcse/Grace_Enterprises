## 🏠 About Grace Enterprises

**Grace Enterprises** is a professional deep-cleaning service business website created to establish a strong digital presence and make it easier for customers to discover services and contact the business.

The website focuses on combining:

- Professional service presentation
- Modern UI design
- Responsive layouts
- Clear pricing information
- Service discovery
- Visual gallery presentation
- Frequently asked questions
- Direct customer enquiries

The result is a practical business website designed for real customers rather than simply a development demonstration.
